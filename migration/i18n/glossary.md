# DMU English → Arabic translation guide

Tone: formal Modern Standard Arabic, as used by UAE universities. Translate meaning naturally (not word-for-word). No explanations or notes.

## Rules
- Keep numbers in Western digits (0-9). Translate month names: January يناير, February فبراير, March مارس, April أبريل, May مايو, June يونيو, July يوليو, August أغسطس, September سبتمبر, October أكتوبر, November نوفمبر, December ديسمبر.
- Keep in Latin script: course codes (e.g. "PHA 101"), acronyms (MD, CAA, ACPE, GPA, IELTS, TOEFL, EmSAT, LMS, MMI), product names (Brightspace, Microsoft Office, Moodle), journal/article titles in references and citations, email addresses and links.
- Person names: write them in Arabic script with the usual Arabic spelling (e.g. "Yousif El-Tayeb" → "يوسف الطيب", "Bassam Atallah" → "بسام عطاالله"). Foreign names: transliterate.
- Organisation names: use the official Arabic name if well known (e.g. Dubai Health Authority → هيئة الصحة بدبي, Cleveland Clinic → كليفلاند كلينك); otherwise translate sensibly.
- Research publication lists / citations (authors, journal names, article titles): leave them in English unchanged.
- Very short fragments (a single word, "and", a label) are pieces of a longer sentence or a table cell: translate them so they read naturally.
- Keep leading/trailing punctuation such as ":", "–", "*", "(", ")" where it makes sense.
